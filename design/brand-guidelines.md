# Brand guidelines

- **Name:** "CareerPilot AI" (working name; the UI reads `app_name` from configuration on the API side, and
  the web wordmark is in `app-shell.tsx`, `auth-card.tsx` and `page.tsx`).
- **Wordmark:** "CareerPilot" in text color + " AI" in primary blue, semibold, tight tracking. SVG:
  [assets/logo.svg](assets/logo.svg).
- **Voice:** plain, specific, calm. Say what happened and what the user can do next. Avoid hype ("magic",
  "perfect match", "guaranteed").
- **AI language:** "suggested", "draft", "relevance". Never "the AI decided". Always say when content came from a
  mock provider.
- **Status language:** "Not selected" rather than "Rejected" for candidates; "Not in your confirmed profile"
  rather than "Missing".
- **Imagery:** none required; icons from Lucide at 16-20 px, always paired with text or an `aria-label`.
